### Long Description (4000 char max)
**PIXREVEAL — A retro-arcade classic, reborn in neon.**

Inspired by the legendary Qix and Gals Panic arcade games of the 80s and 90s, PixReveal brings the addictive territory-capture puzzle to a new generation with a stunning cyberpunk twist.

Move your neon marker along the borders, draw lines into uncharted territory, and seal off areas to reveal stunning hidden artwork beneath the colored overlay. But beware — venomous tarantulas roam the playfield, and one wrong move means losing a life!

🎮 **CLASSIC GAMEPLAY, MODERN POLISH**
Pure grid-based mechanics. No gimmicks, no pay-to-win. Just you, the spiders, and the territory you claim. Every cell matters.

🕷️ **ENEMIES THAT THINK**
Spiders don't just wander — they hunt. When you start drawing your trail, they switch to attack mode and chase you down with deadly precision.

🎨 **5 IMAGE PACKS, 20+ HIGH-QUALITY ARTWORKS**
Reveal stunning 4K images across themed packs:
• Super Cars — exotic supercars and street machines
• Deep Space — galaxies, nebulae and cosmic wonders
• Wild Animals — majestic creatures of nature
• Beach Glamour ⭐ Premium
• Fitness Models ⭐ Premium

⚡ **POWER-UPS**
Discover hidden power-ups in captured zones:
• ❄️ Freeze — stop enemies in their tracks
• 🏃 Speed — boost your movement
• 🛡️ Shield — survive one hit

🏆 **11 LEVELS — INCREASING DIFFICULTY**
Climb through 11 hand-crafted levels of escalating challenge. Master each one to unlock the next, fight the BOSS at Level 10, and earn 1-3 stars based on your performance.

💎 **TOKEN ARCADE SYSTEM**
Just like the old arcade machines — insert a token, get 3 lives. Run out? Watch a rewarded ad, claim your daily login bonus, or buy a token pack. No energy timers, no waiting walls.

🌐 **TURKISH & ENGLISH**
Full localization for Turkish and English players.

🎵 **NEON CYBERPUNK AESTHETICS**
Custom synthwave UI, animated retro fonts, glowing neon trails, and a hand-painted spider enemy that feels alive.

**FREE TO PLAY**
PixReveal is free to download and play. Optional in-app purchases support the developer and unlock premium image packs.

**CONTACT**
Questions, bugs, ideas? Reach out at info@cytbilisim.com
